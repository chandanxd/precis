## 3. Detailed Audit Findings

### ARCH-01 [Medium Severity]: Absence of Cross-Document Structural Chunk Deduplication
- **File**: `backend/src/precis/pipeline/structural.py`, lines 1346–1360
- **Observation**: While intra-document table-list overlaps are deduplicated at ingestion, and SVOA facts are deduplicated against structural evidence, there is no cross-document deduplication among structural chunks.
- **Impact**: If multiple documents contain identical or near-duplicate tables, both chunks can enter `ranked_struct` with equal top scores and consume up to 2 or 3 of the maximum 3 structural slots.
- **Remediation Recommendation**: In a future optimization release, introduce a Jaccard or header-matching deduplication filter among `ranked_struct` candidates before taking the top-3 slice.

### TEST-01 [Low Severity]: Natural Embedding Scores in Integration Ranking Tests
- **File**: `scripts/test_experiment_4_5_persistence_audit.py`
- **Observation**: Tests A, B, and C rely on natural embedding similarities generated by `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` from constructed HTML snippets rather than injecting synthetic score tuples into `query_similar_structural()`.
- **Impact**: While this provides true end-to-end integration validation, tests are sensitive to potential future modifications to embedding weights.
- **Remediation Recommendation**: Supplement with isolated unit tests using synthetic score tuples `[(item1, 0.45), (item2, 0.55)]`.

### DOC-01 [Low Severity]: Descriptive Compression in `implementation_report.md`
- **File**: `results/diagnostic_experiment_4_v1/retrieval_defect_remediation_20261010/implementation_report.md`
- **Observation**: Section 4 described eligibility as a single combined boolean condition, omitting the distinction between Filter 1 (relevance threshold $\ge 0.25$ or keyword match $\ge 0.15$) and Filter 2 (uncorroborated document eligibility threshold $\ge 0.40$).
- **Resolution**: `ranking_strategy.md` documents the explicit two-phase pipeline accurately, eliminating ambiguity.
